Using the app

No account is required. The first launch creates an anonymous session. You can later link Sign in with Apple or Google from Settings › Account › Keep your progress so your treks follow you to a new device.

The app is for people aged 13 and over. Mountains and summits are metaphors for focused time, not fitness tracking.

Restore purchases

On the Summit Pro paywall, tap Restore purchases. The same row is in Settings › Account. Use the Apple Account that bought Summit Pro.

Cancel Summit Pro

On iPhone: Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Focus. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Delete your account

Settings › Account › Delete my account erases your profile, sessions, and public handle from our servers and signs you out. Settings › Account › Export my data first if you want a copy. If you never signed in, use Clear local data on that same screen.

Report or block someone

Open their profile on Camp (or in a shared session) and tap Report or Block. A block is mutual: you disappear from each other’s Camp, Insights, invite codes, and live sessions. You can also email the address above. We review reports within one business day.
