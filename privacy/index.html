Focus: Summit Together

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 September 2026

Focus: Summit Together ("the app") is a focus timer that turns your focused minutes into progress up real mountains. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app handles, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to the Focus: Summit Together app for iOS and Android (shown on your home screen as "Focus") and to the site at https://rishabhDS.github.io/focussummit-site/.

We built the app around a simple rule: the app collects the least it can, keeps it only as long as you keep your account, and never sells or shares it for advertising. There are no ads in the app and no advertising trackers.

1. Who we are

The app is operated by the Focus: Summit Together team ("we", "us"). You can reach us at tiny.thinkers.ds@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

Your account

When you first open the app we create an anonymous account for you using Firebase Authentication (a Google service). This gives you a random account ID so your treks and friends can sync. It is not tied to your name, email or phone number.

You may optionally link Sign in with Apple or Google so you can keep your progress on a new device. If you do, Firebase Authentication stores the email address (or Apple private relay address) your provider shares. We use it only to sign you in. Google may also attach a name and profile photo; we discard both immediately and never show them. Neither email, name nor photo appears in your public profile, in analytics or in anything other users can see. You are never required to link an account to use the app.

What you enter in the app

  • Age confirmation. In onboarding we ask whether you are 13 or older. An anonymous account may already exist so the app can run; if you tell us you are under 13 we delete that account immediately and the app cannot be used. We store only the answer, not a birth date.
  • Handle. A short display name you choose. This is the only thing other users can see about you, along with the trek you are currently on.
  • Custom activity labels stay on your device. If you share a session with a friend, the label is replaced with a generic category before anything leaves your phone.
  • Settings and preferences such as your default session length, sound, haptics and notification choices.

Session records

Each focus session you complete creates a record: when it started, how long you planned, how long you actually focused, which trek it counted towards, and one of a fixed list of activity categories. There is no free-text field. We use these records to compute your progress, streaks and summits, and to show them back to you in Insights.

Focusing with a friend

You can focus alongside a friend by exchanging a short code or link. The code resolves to a shared session, not to a person. Your friend sees your handle and your active trek while you focus together, and you see theirs. The app has no discovery, no matching with strangers and no messaging. You can report or block another user from Camp, from a shared session, or by emailing tiny.thinkers.ds@gmail.com; a block is mutual and removes you from each other's sessions.

Purchases

Summit Pro is an optional subscription sold through the App Store or Google Play. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your card or bank details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your anonymous account ID, a device identifier (IDFV on iOS), and the purchase receipt for this purpose.

Diagnostics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports (device model, OS version, app version, a device identifier, and the technical state of the app at the time of the crash). These help us fix bugs. Crash reports are not linked to your handle or session history.

Usage analytics — only if you say yes

Anonymous usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) are off by default. If you turn on Share anonymous usage data in Settings, we collect events such as "session started" or "paywall dismissed" with coarse, bucketed values. We never send free text, email addresses or precise timings. You can turn this off at any time in one tap; collection stops immediately. Firebase retains previously collected analytics according to its standard period (14 months) unless you also delete your account.

What we do not collect

Precise location, contacts, camera-roll photos (we discard any profile photo Google attaches at sign-in), browsing history, installed-app lists, message content, health data, or advertising identifiers. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Where your data is stored and how long

Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure. It is kept for as long as you keep your account. When you delete your account, your account record, profile, sessions, progress, consents, friend links, public profile, and any reports you filed or that named your account are erased from our servers, and your authentication record is deleted. Backups roll off within 30 days. Analytics and crash data, which are not tied to your handle, are retained by Firebase according to its standard retention periods (analytics: 14 months; crash data: 90 days).

5. Your choices and rights

All of these are available inside the app, no email required:

  • Export your data. Settings › Account › Export my data.
  • Delete your account. Settings › Account › Delete my account.
  • Control analytics. Settings › About › Share anonymous usage data.
  • Block or report another user from their profile in Camp.

Depending on where you live you may also have legal rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data. Email tiny.thinkers.ds@gmail.com; we will respond within 30 days.

6. Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK users)

We process account and session data to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract). Crash diagnostics are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app working. Usage analytics are processed only with your consent.

7. Children

The app is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete it. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, email tiny.thinkers.ds@gmail.com.

8. Security

Data travels over TLS. Server-side rules ensure only you can read your own records. Session checkpoints on your device are stored in the platform keychain or keystore.

9. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the effective date. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
